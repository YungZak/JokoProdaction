About Artem

Joko Production · Est. 2018

Artem Koshevarov is a Charlotte-based cinematographer and photographer working at the intersection of brand storytelling and documentary realism.

Joko Production was founded in 2018 as a one-man studio for clients who care about light, restraint, and the moment a frame stops feeling staged. Comfortable on a gimbal in the woods or on a stage with a 12-light setup, Artem shoots commercials, music videos, brand films, and editorial work across the Carolinas and beyond.

Clients include independent labels, regional agencies, and brands looking for the quieter end of the spectrum — less polish, more presence.
